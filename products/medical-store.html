Who it is for

Owner-run medical stores and small pharmacy groups, especially those using old desktop software or notebooks.

What it does

Fast billing

Keyboard-first selling for a busy counter, with hold bills and receipts.

Loose tablets and batches

Sell by the piece, and track batch and expiry so you see what will expire soon.

Khata and supplier dues

Customer credit and money owed to suppliers in one place, with WhatsApp reminders for overdue khata.

Expenses, staff and profit

Expenses, staff pay and monthly profit, so you know how the shop is really doing.

Reports

Sales trends, best sellers, low margins and expiry risk.

Owner phone app

Watch sales, cash and alerts from your phone, without selling from it.
