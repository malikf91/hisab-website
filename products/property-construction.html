Property & Construction: Deals, projects and investors in one place

For property dealers and builders who work with investors: every purchase, sale, expense and payment recorded in one ledger, with clear reports for the owner. It is in daily use by property and construction businesses.

Ask about Property & Construction on WhatsApp See screenshots

This is working software, used every day by real businesses. Screenshots on this page show a demo with sample data.

Who it is for

Property dealers, developers and builders who handle investor money, several plots or projects, and want to stop piecing it together from notebooks and Excel.

What it does

Properties from purchase to sale

Record each property you buy, the expenses on it, and the sale, with profit worked out for you.

Construction projects

Follow each project from start to finish: contract value, expenses and payments received.

Investors and profit

Keep each investor's capital and share clear, with monthly profit reports. Investors can have their own view of their account.

Loans and trust deposits

Track money lent out and amaanat (trust deposits) so nothing is lost between people.

Staff and expenses

Employees, payments to them, and recurring company expenses such as rent.

Reports the owner can use

A dashboard with cash position and capital deployed, plus monthly progress and investor profit reports you can download as PDF or Excel.

If your records are in notebooks or Excel, we move your properties, investors and balances across and check them with you before you start using it.
