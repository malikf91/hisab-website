Taleem: School management for private schools

Built for private school owners who still run on registers, WhatsApp and Excel: collect fees without chasing, tell parents when a child is absent, and see the whole school from your phone. This is an interactive demo with sample data.

This is an interactive demo with sample data. It shows what the product can do and is not a finished product yet. Tell me what your business needs and we can shape it together.

Who it is for

Owners and principals of private schools, from a single campus to a small chain.

What it does

Fees that get collected

Monthly vouchers for the whole school, a fee desk with receipts, part payments, discounts, and a defaulter list with one-tap WhatsApp reminders.

Absence alerts to parents

When a teacher marks a child absent, the parent is told on WhatsApp, in Urdu or English.

Report cards in minutes

Enter marks once and get grades, positions and printable report cards.

Safe pickup

The guard checks who is collecting the child against the parent's approved list.

Phone apps for everyone

A view for the owner, teachers, parents, guards and van drivers.

Staff and expenses

Staff payroll, vendors and school expenses alongside the fee records.
